How to choose the right service page

Start from the symptom. Gurgling drains and standing water point to drain or sewer work. A wet ceiling, a warm patch on the floor or a water bill that jumps without explanation points to leak detection. Cold showers point to the water heater pages.

Several problems overlap. A slow drain in every fixture usually means the main line, while one slow sink is a local clog. If you cannot tell, call and describe it, because the first question a plumber asks is where the water is going and where it is not.

Licensed work, clear pricing

California licenses plumbing contractors under the C-36 classification, and homeowners can check any licence on the Contractors State License Board website before work starts. Our plumbers are licensed and insured, and the price is in writing before work begins.

Work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Costs depend on access, pipe material, parts and the time of day, so we do not publish one-size prices. Each service page explains the factors that move the price.
