Colton Water

Why Hard Water Shapes Plumbing Here.

The City of Colton water quality report lists average total hardness for the Colton and Rialto supply at 206.67 mg/L. That is about 12 grains per gallon, and it falls in the very hard band of the USGS scale, which starts at 181 mg/L.

Hard water leaves scale on the hottest surfaces. That is why water heaters and faucets in Colton often fail from scale before they wear out. If your heater rumbles or your aerators clog, ask us about water heater replacement or a water softener.

Colton Homes

Older Houses Need Different Care.

City housing data shows that 52% of Colton homes were built before 1980, and another 28.5% were built from 1980 to 1989. Older homes can hide corroded galvanized lines, aging cast iron drains and clay sewer laterals, while newer ones tend to have copper or plastic pipe.

That mix is why a quick fix is not always the right fix, and why all of our work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). A technician checks the pipe material first. Then you hear whether a repair will hold or whether repiping or sewer line repair makes more sense.
