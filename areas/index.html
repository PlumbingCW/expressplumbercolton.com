Colton itself

Colton is home base. Our shop is at 605 E Valley Blvd, and the 92324 ZIP code covers most of the city. Neighborhoods near Valley Boulevard, the I-10 and I-215 interchange and the Agua Mansa area are all on our regular routes.

The City of Colton runs its own water and sewer utilities, so many Colton calls start with a question about which side of the meter or the property line a problem is on. We help sort that out before any repair.

What happens when your street is on the edge

Dispatch decides by address, not by town name. If you live between two of these towns or in an unincorporated pocket, call the 24/7 line and we will confirm coverage and the next available appointment.

Nearby places such as Highgrove, Muscoy and Jurupa Valley are close to our routes, so ask if you are not sure.
