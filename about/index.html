About Colton Express Plumbing

.

Colton Express Plumbing is a plumbing company based at 605 E Valley Blvd in Colton, California. We answer a 24/7 emergency line and send licensed, insured plumbers to Colton and nearby towns.

What we do

We repair and install plumbing for homes and small businesses. That includes drains, sewer lines, leak detection, burst pipes, water heaters, fixtures, repiping and softeners. The full list is on the services page.

Our focus is emergency and same-day work. When a pipe bursts or a drain backs up, the useful thing is a quick answer and a plumber who can start, and that is how the business is set up.

How we price and explain work

Every job starts with a diagnosis, and you get a clear price before work starts. If the scope changes after we open a wall or run a camera, we stop and explain it before anything else happens.

We tell you what is urgent and what can wait. A problem that can safely wait until morning is not a reason to pay for a night call, and we will say so.

Licensing and limits

California licenses plumbing contractors through the Contractors State License Board under the C-36 classification, and you can check any licence on the board's website. Our plumbers are licensed and insured.

Our plumbers work to the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). We do plumbing, not electrical or structural work. If a job needs an electrician, a roofer or a city inspection, we tell you and help you plan around it. For permits, the City of Colton Building Division is the authority.

What a typical emergency call looks like

You call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The dispatcher asks a short set of questions: where the water is coming from, whether it is still running, and whether the main valve is shut. Those answers decide how urgent the visit is and what the plumber should bring.

While you wait, we tell you the safe steps. That usually means closing the main water valve, keeping people away from wet floors near outlets, and moving belongings out of the water path. If you smell gas, we send you outside first and ask you to call the gas utility or 911.

On arrival, the plumber confirms the cause, explains the options and gives you the price in writing. Work starts after you approve. Before leaving, the plumber tests the repair, shows you the shutoff valves and tells you what to watch over the next few days.

How Colton shapes our work

Local conditions change how plumbing fails here. The City of Colton reports an average water hardness of 206.67 mg/L, which is very hard water, so scale is a regular cause of water heater and fixture problems. City housing data shows 52% of homes were built before 1980, so aging pipes and drains come up often.

Those facts mean our plumbers check scale on heaters and faucets, test old supply lines for corrosion and look at drain material before recommending a fix. The City of Colton also runs its own water and sewer utilities, so we help you work out who is responsible for a problem before spending money on it.

What we do not do

We do not diagnose health effects of water, we do not do electrical or structural work, and we do not give legal or insurance advice. Homeowners policies often cover sudden and accidental water damage and often exclude wear, corrosion and slow leaks, so read your policy and document damage before cleanup.

We also do not quote prices over the phone for work we have not seen, because pipe condition and access change the job. You get a clear written price before work starts instead.

How we keep the work honest

Plumbing is a trade where customers cannot easily check the work, so we try to make it visible. We show you the failed part when we remove it, we point out the shutoff valves before we leave, and we write down what was done.

If a cheaper fix will hold, we say so. If it will not, we explain why and let you decide. You can also ask any plumber to wait while you read the price, because nobody should feel rushed into a repair they do not understand.

Where we work

Home base is Colton, and we also serve Grand Terrace, Rialto, San Bernardino, Loma Linda and Bloomington. See the service areas page for details, or contact us to confirm coverage for your address.
