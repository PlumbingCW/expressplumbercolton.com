How to Prevent Clogged Drains in Colton, CA

.
By the Colton Express Plumbing team · Updated October 24, 2026
.

To prevent clogged drains in Colton, keep four things out of your pipes: cooking grease, hair, wipes and anything that is not toilet paper. Add a drain screen to every tub and shower, flush hot water after washing dishes, and clean the traps you can reach once a month. These habits stop most household stoppages before they start.

Colton's very hard water adds a local twist, because minerals and soap together leave a crust that narrows pipes. The sections below cover each habit, what to avoid pouring, and the signs that mean it is time for a plumber.

The monthly drain routine that works

.

Ten minutes once a month beats a weekend with a plunger.

  • Pull the hair. Lift the stopper or screen in each tub and sink and remove the tangle you find. Do this even if the drain flows well.
  • Flush with hot water. Run the hottest tap for a full minute after cleaning to carry loosened film down the line.
  • Clean the trap lip. Wipe the visible edge of the drain opening, where soap and grime collect first.
  • Rinse the aerator and screens. A clogged faucet screen mimics a slow drain and slows the flush that helps rinse the pipe.
  • Run the least used drain. A guest bathroom drain with a dried trap can smell and clog after long idle spells, so run water for a minute.
  • Check under the sink. Look for dampness at slip joints and on the cabinet floor, which are early signs of a partial blockage.

Kitchen grease: the clog that builds silently

.

Liquid grease cools inside the pipe and coats the wall. Each rinse adds another layer, and soon it traps food bits and narrows the opening. A hot water flush may move it a few feet but it hardens again further down, where you cannot reach it.

Pour cooled grease into a can or jar, let it set, then toss it in the trash. Wipe pans with a paper towel before washing. The same goes for coffee grounds, rice and pasta, which swell and settle in the trap.

A garbage disposal grinds food but does not make grease safe to pour.

Why wipes labeled flushable still clog

.

Toilet paper breaks apart in seconds. Wipes are made of fibers that stay together, so they snag on a rough joint, catch hair and form a rope. The word "flushable" on the package describes whether they pass through the toilet, not whether they clear the main line.

If you want one rule for toilets, flush only human waste and toilet paper. A small wastebasket by the toilet solves most problems.

Hair, soap scum and scale in bathroom drains

.

Bathroom drains clog slowly, which is why people notice them late.

Hair catches soap

Hair wraps around the stopper and any rough spot in the pipe. Soap then turns it into a gummy mat that water cannot pass.

Soap plus hard water

Soap reacts with calcium to make a pasty scum. In hard water areas the scum builds up on the pipe wall faster than in soft water.

Use a drain screen

A fine mesh screen over each drain catches hair before it enters the trap. Empty it after each shower.

Skip the bar soap sliver

Tiny soap remnants break up and join the scum in the trap. Use a bath mitt or liquid soap if the drain is slow.

Why chemical drain cleaners cause more trouble than they fix

.

Liquid openers are strong acids or lye. They can sit above a blockage for hours, which heats the pipe, harms rubber seals and leaves hazardous water in the trap for the next person to handle. Mixing two products can release dangerous fumes.

They also do little against hair, grease and wipes, because the chemicals eat soft residue around a clog without removing the clog itself. If you used one and the drain did not clear, tell the plumber. They will wear gloves and goggles before opening the trap.

An enzyme cleaner can help with film buildup as a monthly maintenance product, but it does not clear a hard blockage.

When a drain slows: what to try before calling

.

Start gentle and stop if water is rising.

  1. Check for a single clogged fixture. If only one sink is slow, the problem is likely in the trap or branch line, not the main.
  2. Use a cup plunger. Seal the overflow with a damp cloth, fill with a little water, and push and pull firmly for about twenty seconds.
  3. Clear the trap. Put a bucket under the sink, unscrew the slip nuts, empty the P-trap and rinse it. Reassemble hand tight.
  4. Use a small hand auger. A drain snake can pull out hair near the opening. Do not push hard or force it past a bend.
  5. Stop if the pattern is wider. If other drains gurgle or back up, stop using water and call. Our drain cleaning service handles both single drains and shared lines.
Local note.

Colton housing and water facts that change the routine

.

About 52 percent of Colton's homes were built before 1980, from the City of Colton's housing data. Older drain lines can have rough interiors and slow slopes, so a kitchen line may clog sooner than a newer one. The practical move is a yearly clean-out of kitchen and laundry drains, plus a camera look if one line keeps clogging.

The same City document shows 32.1 percent of units are in multifamily buildings, with 22.5 percent in buildings of five or more units. In a shared building, one tenant's grease or wipes can back up another unit's tub. Report slow drains to your landlord early, since a shared stack often needs a professional with a longer cable.

City water in the Colton/Rialto monitoring table averages near 207 mg/L total hardness. That is very hard on the USGS scale, so expect more soap scum and aerator scale than in soft water areas. Test your tap and ask your water utility for its annual report.

Trap and vent layouts follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), which is why a drain that keeps clogging can point to a venting or slope problem and not only to what went down it.

Signs that the problem is deeper than a trap

.

Several fixtures that slow down together point to the main line. So does a toilet that gurgles when the shower runs, or a floor drain that smells. These are not jobs for monthly maintenance. If water reaches the cleanout cap or a drain starts backing up, our sewer line cleaning team can clear it, and the 24/7 line is open for backups.
