What to have ready when you call

Give the street address, the ZIP code and a short description of what you see. Mention whether water is still running, whether you have shut off the main valve and whether anyone has been near wet electrical areas.

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us for the plumbing side.

Before the plumber arrives

Shut off the main water valve if a pipe is leaking, and keep people away from wet floors near outlets and appliances. Move rugs and valuables out of the water path, and take photos of the damage for your insurance claim.

Our service pages, such as burst pipe repair and drain cleaning, explain the safe steps for each situation.
