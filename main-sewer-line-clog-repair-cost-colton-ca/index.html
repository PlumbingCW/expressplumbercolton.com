Main Sewer Line Clog Repair Cost in Colton, CA

.
By the Colton Express Plumbing team · Updated October 24, 2026
.

A main sewer line clog repair cost in Colton usually lands in one of two buckets. Clearing the blockage with a cable or water jet is the lower bucket. Digging up and replacing broken pipe is the higher one, and the gap between them is large.

What decides the bucket is mostly access, how far the clog sits from the cleanout, whether roots or a collapsed section caused it, and which tool clears it. This guide walks through each factor, shows a published national range with its source, and explains how to tell clearing from repair before anyone quotes you.

Clearing a clog versus repairing the pipe

.

Most price confusion comes from one word, "repair," covering two different jobs. Clearing removes whatever is blocking the pipe. Repair fixes the pipe itself, so the blockage does not come right back.

A plumber can clear grease, paper and soft roots with a cable machine in an hour or two. If a camera then shows a belly, an offset joint or a cracked clay section, the clog was a symptom. That finding moves the job from a service call toward a repair estimate.

This is why a trustworthy quote comes in two steps. First the line is cleared and scoped. Then you get a written price for any repair the video actually supports. If a company quotes a full replacement over the phone with no camera, ask what they based it on.

.

The tool matters less than whether it fits the clog, but each one carries a different cost profile.

Cost figures from a published guide

.

A Fixr cost guide updated in January 2025 puts the national average to unclog a main sewer line at $475, with a typical range of $350 to $650. The same guide lists snaking at $100 to $500 and hydro jetting at $350 to $600. It also notes that about $400 of that average is labor.

On the repair side, the guide lists section replacement at $50 to $250 per linear foot, and a camera inspection at $100 to $800. Installing a new sewer cleanout runs $600 to $2,000 in the same source. These are national numbers, not a Colton price list, and they do not include permits or surface restoration.

Treat them as a sanity check. Your written price from Colton Express Plumbing comes before work starts and reflects what the plumber finds on your line.

Signs the clog is in the main line, not one drain

.

A cost conversation only makes sense once you know which pipe is blocked.

  • More than one fixture is slow. If the shower, a toilet and the washing machine drain all misbehave together, the shared line is the likely culprit.
  • The lowest drain complains first. A tub or floor drain on the lowest level often burps or backs up before anything upstairs does.
  • Flushing makes another fixture gurgle. Air and water are being pushed back through the nearest open vent path because the main cannot take the flow.
  • Water shows at the cleanout. If a cleanout cap outside is wet or the pipe stands full, the blockage is downstream of that point.
  • Clearing one sink did nothing. A cleared trap that still leaves the house sluggish points past the branch line.

Access and distance: the quiet price drivers

.

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives a plumber a straight shot into the line. With one, a cable machine goes in quickly and the crew works at full speed. Without one, the tech may have to pull a toilet or reach in through a roof vent, which adds labor and some risk to the fixture.

Distance matters because cables and jetting hoses only reach so far, and the clog's location sets how long the run is. A blockage 20 feet out in the yard is a different job from one near the street connection. Camera footage gives the footage count, which also helps size any repair.

If a cleanout is missing, asking for one to be added during the visit is reasonable. It costs more today and saves labor on every later call.

Why roots change the whole estimate

.

Roots find the tiny gaps at joints, grow inside the pipe, and catch paper and grease like a net. A cutter head clears the mass in one visit, but the roots return unless the joint is sealed or the pipe is lined or replaced.

That is why a root-driven clog can be cheap once and expensive across a year. Ask your plumber to show the video of the joint, then decide between scheduled cutting and a permanent fix.

What drives a written sewer quote

.

These are the factors a plumber weighs before writing a number. None of them can be priced fairly over the phone.

Access point An existing cleanout is faster than working through a fixture or roof vent.
Length and depth A longer run or a deeper line adds labor, equipment time and excavation if repair is needed.
Cause of the stoppage Grease and paper are routine. Roots, offsets and collapsed pipe call for more work.
Method and passes Cable, jetting and camera work can be combined, and each added tool adds time.
Repair scope and restoration Digging through concrete, landscaping or a driveway adds surface work, permits and utility marking.
Homeowners policies often cover sudden and accidental damage, such as a backup that ruins flooring. They often exclude wear, corrosion, root intrusion and slow leaks. Read your policy, take photos before cleanup, and keep the camera video if one was recorded.
A clear written price comes before work starts. If the camera finds more than a clog, you decide on the repair after seeing the footage.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

.

These steps limit damage and give the tech a faster start.

  1. Stop using water. Hold off on flushing, laundry and the dishwasher, since every gallon goes to the same blocked line.
  2. Find the cleanout. Look for a capped pipe near the house or along a side wall, and clear space around it without opening it.
  3. Protect floors and belongings. Move rugs and boxes away from the lowest drains and put towels at the base of tubs and floor drains.
  4. Write down what happened. Note which fixtures backed up and when, since that pattern helps the tech locate the blockage.
  5. Skip chemical drain openers. They sit in a full line, can burn skin, and make the cleanup more hazardous for whoever opens the line.
Local note.

Colton details that affect a sewer job

.

About 52 percent of Colton homes were built before 1980, based on the City of Colton's 2021 housing data. Older laterals are more likely to have joints that let roots in. We cannot say what pipe your house has, so ask the plumber to record a camera pass and note the pipe type on screen.

If a repair means digging, California law requires a call to 811 or DigAlert at least two working days before the ground is opened. That timing can add a day or two to a repair that is not an emergency clearing, so plan around it.

Pipe replacement may also need a permit. We do not state which repairs trigger one, so ask the City of Colton Building Division before work begins. Also ask which agency maintains the public sewer main in your street, because a clog past your property line is not a private repair. Sewer lateral work is governed by the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so a new cleanout or a pipe replacement is code work.

When to call instead of trying it yourself

.

A rented drum machine can clear a simple stoppage, but a main line is not a forgiving place to learn. A cable can hang up in a joint, break inside the pipe or chew an old clay section. That turns a clearing into a repair, and you now own the bill.

Call a plumber for repeat backups, sewage in a tub or floor drain, a wet cleanout, or any odor that persists after drains are cleared. The 24/7 line is open for backups that cannot wait.
