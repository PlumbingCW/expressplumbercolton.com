Privacy Policy

.

This policy explains what information the Colton Express Plumbing website collects and how it is used. It was last updated on October 24, 2026.

..

What this website collects

The site has no contact forms, accounts or checkout, so it does not ask you to type personal details into a page. The web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell personal information.

Calls and email

When you call or email us, we collect the details you share, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem. We use them to schedule and perform the service you asked for, to follow up on that service and to keep records of the work.

Calls may be recorded or logged for quality and training. If a recording notice applies, it is given at the start of the call.

Third-party content

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive your IP address and browser details when the content loads, under Google's own privacy policy.

We do not run advertising pixels on the site pages.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing the address on the contact page. California residents may have additional rights under state privacy law, and we respond to valid requests as the law requires.

We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows the latest version.
